NOPIXEL GIVEAWAY CLICKER — QUICK START

1. Extract the downloaded ZIP.
2. Run Create Desktop Shortcut.vbs in the extracted folder.
3. Drag NoPixel Giveaway Clicker.lnk to your Desktop.
4. Open the app with the shortcut, then click Open Edge.
5. Sign into Twitch in the new Edge window. This is the app's separate
   browser profile; your regular Edge profile is not used.
6. Enter Twitch channel names (comma or space separated) and click Open
   channels. You can also open channels manually in that Edge window.
7. Click Start monitoring.

Stop pauses giveaway monitoring. Wake tabs now briefly brings background
channel tabs forward. Periodic wake is optional and off by default.

Keep the app and its Edge window open. Your sign-in, channel counts, and app
profile are stored locally under %LOCALAPPDATA%\CardClicker.

Microsoft Edge is required. No browser extension, Python, or Command Prompt
is required for this package. For details, see README.md.
