AUTO CLICKER — QUICK START

1. Right-click Auto Clicker.zip and choose Extract All. Click Extract.
2. Open the extracted Auto Clicker folder.
3. Double-click Create Desktop Shortcut.vbs. This creates Auto Clicker.lnk
   in the same folder.
4. Drag Auto Clicker.lnk to your Desktop.
5. Double-click the Desktop shortcut to open the app.

Keep the extracted Auto Clicker folder where it is. The Desktop shortcut
points to the app inside that folder. If you move the folder, create a new
shortcut from Auto Clicker.exe.

The app starts stopped. Click Start to scan and click at most one target
immediately, then repeat every 10 seconds. Click Stop to pause. The screen
preview marks detected targets; the mask preview shows matching pixels.

No Python, pip, Command Prompt, or installation is needed for this package.
